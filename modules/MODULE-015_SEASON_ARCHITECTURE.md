# MODULE-015 — Season Architecture

**Status:** BUILD
**Depends on:** 012,014

## Objective
Turn the Series Bible into a macro escalation plan across the requested episode count.

## Requirements
- Define season phases, major turns, midpoint, climax, resolution direction and escalation curve.
- Allocate character-arc progression and production complexity.
- Persist/version the season plan.
- Ensure episode count/duration constraints come from CreativeBrief.

## Verification
Schema, persistence and generation tests across short and long seasons.

## Done when
Episode outlines are generated from a coherent season spine rather than independently; commit/push.
