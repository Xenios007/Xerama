# MODULE-020 — Story Quality Engine

**Status:** EXTEND
**Depends on:** 018,019

## Objective
Score microdrama retention and production quality before media spend.

## Requirements
- Evaluate first-3-second hook, conflict curve, emotional intensity, information gaps, reversal density, climax placement, cliffhanger, repetition and feasibility.
- Combine deterministic heuristics with optional critic model.
- Persist score components and repair recommendations.
- Define configurable PASS/WARN/BLOCK thresholds.

## Verification
Golden-script fixtures for strong/flat/front-loaded/no-climax cases.

## Done when
Weak episodes can be identified and selectively rewritten before visual production; commit/push.
