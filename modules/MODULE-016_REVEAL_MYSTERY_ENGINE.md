# MODULE-016 — Reveal / Mystery Engine

**Status:** BUILD
**Depends on:** 014,015

## Objective
Schedule secrets, clues, false assumptions, reveals and payoffs to maximize serialized retention without contradictions.

## Requirements
- Model secret owner, who knows, audience knowledge, clue prerequisites, reveal window and payoff.
- Prevent premature reveals and repeated reveals.
- Track promises/payoffs and unresolved questions.
- Feed allowed reveal state into episode generation.

## Verification
Knowledge graph, reveal-order, duplicate reveal and payoff tests.

## Done when
The system can answer what each character/audience may know at any episode; commit/push.
